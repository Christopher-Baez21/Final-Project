Troll Face

History: Trollface was created in September 2008 by 18-year-old student Carlos Ramirez using Microsoft Paint. Originally posted on DeviantArt to mock pointless internet arguments, the smug, grinning caricature quickly became the face of online "trolls" and the cornerstone of early-2010s "Rage Comics." Ramirez copyrighted the design, earning over $100,000 in licensing fees before the meme's mainstream popularity faded. However, it experienced a massive, darker resurgence in 2020 through surreal "Trollge" horror creepypastas, cementing its status as an immortal icon of internet culture.

Uses: Trollface is a famous internet icon used to show online pranks, trickery, and sarcasm. People use it in rage comics, image macros, and comment sections to gloat after tricking someone, mock absurd arguments, share fake "troll physics", or express chaotic internet irony.

Impact: Created by Carlos Ramirez in 2008, the Trollface meme revolutionized internet culture as the universal visual shorthand for online trolling, sarcasm, and mischief. As a cornerstone of early Rage Comics, the mischievous, grinning character popularized modern digital humor, generated over $100,000 in licensing fees, and cemented its legacy as the "father of memes." Its massive cultural impact spanned from forum culture and mainstream television to global political protests, and the iconic face continues to thrive today through surrealist horror adaptations and viral TikTok trends.
